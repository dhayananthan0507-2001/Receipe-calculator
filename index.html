🌿 About Homies Kit

Homies Kit is your food business's production and costing workspace — everything your team needs to run recipes accurately and price them with confidence, in one shared, cloud-backed place instead of scattered notebooks and phone photos.

📦 One product catalogueEvery recipe, ingredient list and Tamil name your whole team can see and search — added once, available everywhere.
🧮 Accurate batch scalingType a batch size and instantly get the exact ingredient quantities, whether you're measuring by weight or volume.
💰 Real production costsIngredient prices, packaging, labour and gas roll up into a true cost per batch and per pack size, so you can price with confidence.
👥 Built for teamsInvite workers with a one-time code, keep pricing edits with the owner, and give everyone the same up-to-date data.
